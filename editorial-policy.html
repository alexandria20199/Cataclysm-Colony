HOW WE PUBLISH

Sharp stories.
Respectful newsroom.

Cataclysm Colony welcomes humor, strong opinions, and unusual stories. These standards keep submissions readable, responsible, and safe for the people in them.

OUR STANDARDCritique ideas and actions. Don’t target people for who they are.
01 / THE BASELINE

What belongs here

01

Be clear

Separate facts from jokes, opinion, and speculation. Don’t present invented events as verified news.

02

Be fair

Criticize public claims and behavior without turning a person’s identity or private life into a punchline.

03

Protect privacy

Leave out private addresses, phone numbers, passwords, private messages, and other identifying details shared without consent.

02 / NOT ALLOWED

Language and content we remove

These rules apply in headlines, articles, comments, profiles, images, and chat.

A

Slurs and identity-based attacks

No slurs, dehumanizing language, or insults targeting a person's identity, background, faith, disability, gender, or orientation.

B

Threats and encouragement of harm

No threats, praise of real-world violence, or language urging someone to hurt themselves or another person.

C

Targeted harassment

No repeated insults, humiliation campaigns, stalking, brigading, or calls for others to attack a person.

D

Private or identifying information

No doxxing, account credentials, private contact details, or personal information shared to intimidate or expose someone.

E

Exploitation and hateful advocacy

No exploitation, adult content involving minors, or material promoting persecution or exclusion of a protected group.

F

Scams and dangerous instructions

No fraud, impersonation, malware, or step-by-step instructions intended to cause real-world harm.

EDITORIAL REVIEW

Context matters.
Safety comes first.

Editors may reject a submission, ask for changes, remove prohibited material, or restrict an account when content breaks these standards. Satire is welcome; using “it was a joke” to excuse threats, slurs, or harassment is not.

03 / BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

If a line needs a real person to be humiliated, exposed, or threatened for it to land, rewrite the line.
